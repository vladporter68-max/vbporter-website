About the Firm

A premier NYC commercial real estate broker.

VB Porter & Company is a leading full-service real estate firm located in the heart of New York City. We operate in the best interests of landlords, tenants, and investors by offering integrated solutions, consultations, and a wide range of real estate services.

Our focus is on providing an individual approach to every client, delivering effective results at every stage of the real estate process. We guarantee the most current and accurate information, which serves as the base for making optimal and timely decisions.

Whether your focus is on real estate purchase, sale, or rental — on commercial properties or on residential condos and co-ops — our staff of real estate brokers and consultants has the expertise to help you find the ideal solution. From office space to retail locations to land development sites, we help you make the right investment.
